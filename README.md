# A-CRM-APPLICATIONFOR-THE-CHAI-SHOP-

## Project: A CRM Application for TheChaiShop&Co

### Team
SHARMILA SRI T, VIDHYA C, VINDHIYA P, VICHITHRA P, VIDHYASHREE S

### Overview
Salesforce-based CRM to manage Customer, Employer and Enterprise data. Automates order quantity, total bill amount calculation and birthday email notifications.

### Purpose
- Streamline CRM
- Automate billing
- Improve data accuracy
- Reports & Dashboards for decision making
- Email automation

### Salesforce Modules Implemented
1. Developer Account Creation
2. Custom Objects & Tabs
3. Lightning App - TheChaiShop&Co
4. Fields, Relationships, Global Picklist
5. Page Layouts & Record Types (Online/Offline Customers)
6. Validation Rules
7. Email Templates & Flows
8. Reports and Dashboards

### Future Scope
Mobile App, AI prediction, WhatsApp alerts
